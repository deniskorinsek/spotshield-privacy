Privacy Policy – SpotShield Birder

Effective date: 12 October 2026

App: SpotShield Birder for Android (package si.spotshield.birder)

Developer: Denis Korinsek, korinsekdenis@gmail.com

Summary

SpotShield Birder does not collect, share or sell personal data. Your photos are processed only on your device. The app has no internet permission, no user account and no analytics, and it does not show ads.

What the app does with your photos

  • You choose a photo (Android photo picker, "Check original", or sharing a photo from another app) or take one with the SpotShield camera.
  • While you work on it, SpotShield keeps a private working copy inside the app and checks it on your device for things that could reveal a location: location data in the file, text, possible vehicle plates, screens, QR codes and barcodes, buildings, bridges and skylines. All checks run on the device with models built into the app.
  • When you create a protected copy, SpotShield writes a new image without location data and hidden file info, then checks the finished file again.
  • Your original photo is never changed.
  • Nothing is uploaded. SpotShield cannot connect to the internet, because the app does not have Android's internet permission.

What is stored on your device, and for how long

  • The private working copy and your edits are kept in the app's private storage so you can continue later. They are deleted when you tap Done or confirm deletion in the app. On the next launch after 24 hours, the app marks unfinished work as expired and asks you to delete it; for a photo taken in SpotShield, it also offers to save the original first. Files remain in private storage until you confirm deletion. There is no background deletion timer.
  • If SpotShield was closed unexpectedly (for example, the phone restarted) and finds more unfinished photos than it can continue with, it keeps them in private storage and asks you whether to save them to your gallery or delete them. They stay until you choose.
  • Protected copies are saved to your gallery (Pictures/SpotShield) only when you choose Save. You can delete them like any other photo.
  • Photos taken with the SpotShield camera stay inside the app until you create a protected copy. Saving the original as well is off by default.
  • To let the receiving app finish reading, SpotShield keeps a separate protected copy prepared for sharing in private storage for at least 24 hours, then deletes it on a later app launch. This copy survives Done and contains no original photo. There is no background deletion timer.
  • Settings such as appearance and language are stored on your device.
  • App data is excluded from Android backups.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet hands the protected copy to the app you pick. From then on, that app's privacy policy applies, and the people you share with can see the photo.

Permissions

  • Camera – to take photos inside the app. Optional.
  • Access to photo location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) – used only by "Check original", so SpotShield can read the location data stored in your original file and tell you about it. The location is never sent anywhere.

The app does not request internet access, contacts, the microphone or your device's location.

Third-party components

SpotShield uses on-device libraries and models, including Google ML Kit (text recognition and barcode scanning, bundled in the app), ONNX Runtime and open-source image models. They run inside the app on your device. Because the app has no internet permission, these components cannot send data off your device. After you save or share a few protected copies, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating window; Google Play shows it and handles your rating, and SpotShield doesn't see it.

Children

SpotShield is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Pravilnik o zasebnosti – SpotShield Birder

Velja od: 12. oktobra 2026

Aplikacija: SpotShield Birder za Android (paket si.spotshield.birder)

Razvijalec: Denis Korinsek, korinsekdenis@gmail.com

Povzetek

SpotShield Birder ne zbira, ne deli in ne prodaja osebnih podatkov. Fotografije se obdelajo samo na tvoji napravi. Aplikacija nima dovoljenja za internet, nima uporabniškega računa, ne zbira analitike in ne prikazuje oglasov.

Kaj aplikacija naredi s tvojimi fotografijami

  • Fotografijo izbereš (Androidov izbirnik fotografij, »Preveri izvirnik« ali deljenje fotografije iz druge aplikacije) ali jo posnameš s kamero SpotShield.
  • Med delom SpotShield v aplikaciji hrani zasebno delovno kopijo in jo na napravi preveri za vse, kar bi lahko razkrilo lokacijo: podatke o lokaciji v datoteki, besedilo, možne registrske tablice, zaslone, kode QR in črtne kode, stavbe, mostove in obrise gora. Vsa preverjanja tečejo na napravi z modeli, vgrajenimi v aplikacijo.
  • Ko ustvariš zaščiteno kopijo, SpotShield zapiše novo sliko brez podatkov o lokaciji in skritih podatkov datoteke ter končano datoteko še enkrat preveri.
  • Izvirna fotografija se nikoli ne spremeni.
  • Nič se ne naloži na splet. SpotShield se ne more povezati z internetom, ker aplikacija nima Androidovega dovoljenja za internet.

Kaj se shrani na napravi in kako dolgo

  • Zasebna delovna kopija in tvoji popravki se hranijo v zasebnem prostoru aplikacije, da lahko nadaljuješ pozneje. Izbrišejo se, ko tapneš Končano ali potrdiš izbris v aplikaciji. Ob naslednjem zagonu po 24 urah aplikacija označi nedokončano delo kot poteklo in te pozove k izbrisu; pri fotografiji, posneti v SpotShieldu, ponudi tudi shranjevanje izvirnika pred izbrisom. Datoteke ostanejo v zasebnem prostoru, dokler ne potrdiš izbrisa. V ozadju ni časovnika za samodejni izbris.
  • Če se je SpotShield nepričakovano zaprl (na primer ob ponovnem zagonu naprave) in najde več nedokončanih fotografij, kot jih lahko nadaljuje, jih hrani v zasebnem prostoru aplikacije in te vpraša, ali jih shraniš v galerijo ali izbrišeš. Ostanejo tam, dokler se ne odločiš.
  • Zaščitene kopije se shranijo v galerijo (Pictures/SpotShield) samo, če izbereš Shrani. Izbrišeš jih lahko kot vsako drugo fotografijo.
  • Fotografije, posnete s kamero SpotShield, ostanejo v aplikaciji, dokler ne ustvariš zaščitene kopije. Shranjevanje izvirnika je privzeto izklopljeno.
  • Da lahko prejemna aplikacija prebere fotografijo do konca, SpotShield ločeno zaščiteno kopijo, pripravljeno za deljenje, hrani v zasebnem prostoru vsaj 24 ur, nato jo izbriše ob poznejšem zagonu aplikacije. Ta kopija ostane po izbiri Končano in ne vsebuje izvirne fotografije. V ozadju ni časovnika za samodejni izbris.
  • Nastavitve, kot sta videz in jezik, se shranijo na napravi.
  • Podatki aplikacije niso vključeni v Androidove varnostne kopije.

Deljenje

Ko tapneš Deli, Androidov meni za deljenje preda zaščiteno kopijo aplikaciji, ki jo izbereš. Od tam naprej velja pravilnik o zasebnosti te aplikacije, fotografijo pa vidijo ljudje, s katerimi jo deliš.

Dovoljenja

  • Kamera – za fotografiranje v aplikaciji. Neobvezno.
  • Dostop do lokacije fotografij (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION) – uporablja ga samo »Preveri izvirnik«, da SpotShield prebere podatke o lokaciji v izvirni datoteki in ti jih pokaže. Lokacija se nikamor ne pošlje.

Aplikacija ne zahteva dostopa do interneta, stikov, mikrofona ali lokacije naprave.

Komponente tretjih oseb

SpotShield uporablja knjižnice in modele, ki tečejo na napravi, med njimi Google ML Kit (prepoznavanje besedila in branje kod, vgrajeno v aplikacijo), ONNX Runtime in odprtokodne modele za slike. Tečejo v aplikaciji na tvoji napravi. Ker aplikacija nima dovoljenja za internet, te komponente ne morejo poslati podatkov z naprave. Ko shraniš ali deliš nekaj zaščitenih kopij, lahko aplikacija Google Play prosi, naj prikaže okno za oceno; okno prikaže in oceno obdela Google Play, SpotShield je ne vidi.

Otroci

SpotShield ni namenjen otrokom, mlajšim od 13 let.
